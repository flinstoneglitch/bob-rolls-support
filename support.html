Bob Rolls Initiative is a survival roguelike set on a tabletop battle map. Struck by lightning mid-campaign, Bob wakes up two inches tall on his own game table and has to survive the horde, one d20 roll at a time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the settings?

Start a run and tap the pause button in the top-right corner, then tap Settings. You'll find sound, music, haptics, the tilt-shift lens, graphics quality and your high scores there.

I don't hear any sound.

First, turn up your iPhone's volume. Then check that Sound Effects and Music are on in Settings. The game plays sound even when your phone is on silent, unless you've turned Play on Silent off. Either way, it mixes with your own music or podcasts.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop from the main menu, go to the Treasury tab, and tap Restore Purchases. One-time purchases and an active VIP Table Seat subscription are restored to the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel VIP Table Seat?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Your progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app erases it. One-time purchases and an active subscription can be restored afterward, but gold and gems can't be recovered.

How does the d20 work?

Every level-up rolls a d20. Roll 15 or higher to choose from four upgrades instead of three. A natural 20 gives you a bonus pick. A natural 1 hits you with a short, silly curse. Raising Fortune on your Character Sheet gives you a chance to roll with advantage: two dice, keep the higher.

Why are there ads?

Ads in Bob Rolls Initiative are always optional. Buttons marked (Ad) let you watch a short video for a bonus, like double gold, a d20 reroll, or a revive. There are no ads you have to sit through. Buying Remove Ads or subscribing to VIP Table Seat grants those bonuses without watching anything.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

To change whether ads can use your device's Advertising Identifier, go to your iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you can also pause a run, open Settings, and tap Ad Choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game plays offline. You only need a connection for optional ads and for making or restoring purchases.
