An iPhone game by Colin O'Reilly

Bob Rolls Initiative

Tabletop roguelike survival

Bob is a middle-aged accountant with thirty years of tabletop gaming under his belt. One stormy game night, lightning strikes mid-session. Bob wakes up two inches tall on his own battle table, and every miniature in the box is coming for him.

Roll for it. Every level-up is a d20 roll. A natural 20 gives you a bonus upgrade; a natural 1 gives you a curse.

Build your Bob. Five weapons, three pieces of nerd gear, and four evolutions, including the Laminated Excalibur.

Survive the horde. Goblins, bats, skeletons and lizardmen by the hundred, then the Red Wyrm at the ten-minute mark.
