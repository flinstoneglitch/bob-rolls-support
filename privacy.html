Bob Rolls Initiative

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Bob Rolls Initiative ("the App") is developed by Colin O'Reilly ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

The short version

We don't collect any personal information ourselves. The App has no accounts and no logins, and your game progress stays on your device. The App shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage information to serve those ads, as described below. Watching an ad is always your choice.

Information We Collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, contact details, location, identifiers, or usage data, and we have no servers that receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some rewards in the game, such as doubling your gold, rerolling a level-up, or reviving, can be earned by choosing to watch a video ad. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When the ad service is active, Google may collect:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device identifiers: Apple's Advertising Identifier (only if you allow tracking) and identifiers specific to this app or developer.
  • Advertising and interaction data, such as which ads were shown, taps, video views, and app launches.
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs, app launch time, hang rate, and energy use.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads (including personalized ads, if you allow them), for analytics, and to prevent fraud. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your Choices

  • Tracking permission. The App uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to ask whether ads may use your device's Advertising Identifier to be more relevant to you. If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized using that identifier, and your rewards and gameplay are the same either way. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are personalized. You can review or change them at any time from the game: pause a run, open Settings, and tap Ad Choices.
  • Remove Ads and VIP Table Seat. If you buy Remove Ads or subscribe to VIP Table Seat, rewards are granted without ads. From the next launch, the App no longer starts the ad service at all.

Data Stored on Your Device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App saves the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Your game progress: gold, gems, upgrades, unlocked cosmetics, Adventure Pass progress, daily quests and login streak.
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, graphics quality) and your high scores.
  • A small gameplay log used to keep rewards balanced. You can see it in the Files app; it is never sent anywhere.

This data never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Deleting the App deletes it.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All purchases, including the optional VIP Table Seat subscription, are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account, or payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Notifications

After your first run, the App may ask whether you'd like a reminder when your free daily chest is ready. The system permission prompt only appears if you say yes. These reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob, described above, is the only third-party service in the App. We don't use any third-party analytics services, and we don't sell your information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
