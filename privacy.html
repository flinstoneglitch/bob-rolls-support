Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Bob Rolls Initiative ("the App") is developed by Colin O'Reilly ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

The short version

We don't collect any personal information. The App has no accounts, no logins, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising services. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Data We Collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, contact details, location, identifiers, or usage data.

Data Stored on Your Device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App saves the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Your game progress: gold, gems, upgrades, unlocked cosmetics, Adventure Pass progress, daily quests and login streak.
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, graphics quality) and your high scores.
  • A small gameplay log used to keep rewards balanced. You can see it in the Files app; it is never sent anywhere.

This data never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Deleting the App deletes it.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All purchases, including the optional VIP Table Seat subscription, are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email address, Apple Account, or payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Notifications

After your first run, the App may ask whether you'd like a reminder when your free daily chest is ready. The system permission prompt only appears if you say yes. These reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
